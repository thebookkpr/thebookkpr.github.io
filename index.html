A Note from the Book Keeper

Welcome. You have found the place.

I keep a small library beneath these roots, where the little stories live: letters, maps, gardens, keepsakes, and quiet adventures. I mend what is worn, and I shelve what is new.

If you do not see me, do not worry. Look for the open book, the warm teacup, the lit lantern. I have only just stepped into another room.

The round green door is never locked.
